Questions, answered

When does PLUG launch?

We're onboarding founding stores in Toronto now and opening to shoppers once the first 50 stores are verified and live. Waitlist members get in first.

How do you stop fakes?

Two layers. Every store is verified before it can list — ID, business or resale proof, and photos of real inventory. Then every payment sits in escrow until delivery is confirmed, so there's a real consequence for bad actors.

What does it cost a store?

A monthly subscription from $49 to $399 depending on the tier, plus 5–9% of each sale. Higher tiers pay a lower cut. Founding stores get their first three months free.

What does it cost a shopper?

Nothing to browse or join. At checkout there's a small buyer protection fee that funds the guarantee on your order.

Which cities are next?

Toronto first, then the next cities are decided by waitlist density — so signing up genuinely moves your city up the list.
